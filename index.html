We adhere to the following digital advertising and privacy frameworks:

SKAdNetwork (SKAN): We use SKAN to measure the conversion rate of ad campaigns while protecting user privacy, providing aggregated and anonymized data to improve ad effectiveness without linking the data to your identity.

Our apps are committed to complying with the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California's California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), reflecting our commitment to protecting the privacy and data of all users. Here's how we ensure compliance:

GDPR: If you are an EU resident, you have the right to access, correct, transfer, or delete your personal information that we have collected. Additionally, you have the right to restrict or object to our processing of your data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly.

Please contact us to exercise these rights.

We take steps to identify and apply relevant privacy laws to your data processing activities and to ensure that your rights are respected and enforceable.
